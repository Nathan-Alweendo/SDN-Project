# Domain Definitions

## Activity
A structured STEMMate learning unit with a title, description, and resource links.

## SessionPlan
A facilitator’s prepared schedule of activities, including time slots and objectives.

## SyncQueue
A temporary storage list that holds unsent updates when the device is offline.

## KitRequest
A record of materials or resources requested by a facilitator for a session.

## ParticipationCount
A numeric record of how many learners attended, stored only as integers.
**FR-08 Compliance:** No text fields, photo uploads, or personal identifiers are allowed.
